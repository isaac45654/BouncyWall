How to Play

1. Touch the black ball and drag in the opposite direction to where you want it to travel.

2. Release to launch the ball.

3. The ball bounces off the outside walls and the obstacle walls.

4. Reach the hole using the maximum number of bounces or fewer.

For example, if the level says 4 bounces, reaching the hole after 0, 1, 2, 3 or 4 bounces is okay.

If you go over the maximum, the level is lost.

If you hit a spike, you die.

You can teleport through portals.

Cobwebs slow you down.

Boosters speed you up.
